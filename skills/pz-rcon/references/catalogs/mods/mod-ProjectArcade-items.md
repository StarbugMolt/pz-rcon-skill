# ProjectArcade - Project Arcade [B42MP]

Workshop ID: 3645980077
Mod ID: ProjectArcade
Enabled on server: yes (B42.13+ MP only)

Arcades are back. Playable retro arcade + pinball machines with original sounds. CLAWpocalypse claw machine exchanges a coin for prizes from a rarity-pool (vanilla plushies, Big Spiffo, Star Pillow, Spiffo suit parts, "I Love Pixels" wallet, DK pen, Blue Ghost keyring, Kate's photo as legendary). Strength/punch scoring machine with server-wide leaderboard (male/female categories).

## Items

**No addable item-prefix to `additem`.** Players obtain coins by:
- Killing zombies
- Toy-store / children's-bedroom loot
- Forging coins (recipe exists)

Coins then drive the claw machine and punch machine.

## Notes
- Spawned machines require ROMs found in electronics locations to build.
- Sandbox option to replace vanilla arcades with this mod's arcades.
- Compatibility addons exist (Spiffomon3D etc.) — author invites other creators to plug into CLAWpocalypse pool.
- **DO NOT** remove the mod midgame without first removing all machines from the world — causes invisible furniture + WorldDictionary errors.

## Use Cases (SIMON voice)
- **Boredom / stress relief** story beats — survivors decompressing at an arcade
- "Kate's photo" = legendary-tier narrative drop (impossible rarity, claw machine)
- Punching-machine high-score bragging rights — SIMON can read top scores from server logs and broadcast them
- "Coin shortage" economic arc — players forging coins to feed their habit
- Companion mod to **HereGoesTheSun** (same author, links in description)
