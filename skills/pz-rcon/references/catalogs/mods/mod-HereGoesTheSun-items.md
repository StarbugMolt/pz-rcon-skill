# HereGoesTheSun - Here Goes the Sun [B42MP]

Workshop ID: 3618557184
Mod ID: HereGoesTheSun
Enabled on server: yes (B42 MP, mid-save safe)

Lighting/atmosphere overhaul. Redesigned sunrises & sunsets with seasonal + weather-based palettes. Spring/summer brighter, autumn golden/brown/muted, winter icy/grey/deep-blue. Storms = muted, dim, cinematic claustrophobia. Includes SportXAI's god rays. Fully deterministic (no random noise). Compatible with other weather mods (load last for best results).

## Items
**No addable items.** Pure atmosphere/lighting mod. SIMON's ambient broadcasts benefit from the visual mood.

## Notes
- Designed for screenshot moments — every season has at least one morning variation
- Future ideas: chromatic nighttime moods, lunar/solar eclipses, more daily variations
- Same author as **ProjectArcade** (companion mod)
- Safe for old and new saves (no new world required)

## Use Cases (SIMON voice)
- **Ambient mood narration**: SIMON's broadcasts can reference specific sky palettes — "golden hour tonight. If you're on a roof, *look up*."
- **Storm cinematic moments**: when SIMON triggers `storm` RCON, this mod makes it *feel* like a storm — perfect backdrop for dramatic broadcasts
- **Seasonal flavor**: SIMON can adapt broadcasts to the in-game season's color tone
- **Eclipse events** (future): SIMON could narrate an eclipse as a "rare signal from above"
