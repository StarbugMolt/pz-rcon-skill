# OCsPacking - Hoarder's Delight: Box & Carton Bulk Packing [B42.20]

Workshop ID: 3626823538
Mod ID: OCsPacking
Enabled on server: yes (B42.12–B42.20, SP & MP)

300+ new boxes and cartons for bulk storage. Packing reduces encumbrance by 1/6 (12kg of items → 10kg box). Stacks: item → box → carton. Safe to add midgame.

## Items

**No new item-prefix to `additem`.** The 300+ boxes are *crafted from vanilla items* — they don't appear with `OCsPacking.X` IDs. Packed items retain their vanilla ID.

Crafting reductions SIMON can reference narratively:
- Most materials
- Most consumable medical supplies
- Most non-perishable food
- Most dried herbs
- Most growable food items

## Notes
- New boxes do **NOT** spawn in vanilla loot tables — crafted only. Loot balance unaffected.
- Packed-food age works like vanilla egg cartons (whole food only).
- Currently investigating fluid container support (bleach, soda).
- Credit to **Vanilla Foods Expanded** for code that plugged some items into vanilla packing recipes.

## Use Cases (SIMON voice)
- **"Hoarder's nightmare" flavor** — survivors packing their bases tighter and tighter
- Encumbrance-reduction arc — "you can carry more if you pack it right"
- Box-of-bullets / box-of-medical supplies as a "tidy stash" reward after a base-defense event
