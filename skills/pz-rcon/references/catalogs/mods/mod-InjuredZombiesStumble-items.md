# InjuredZombiesStumble - Injured Zombies Stumble

Workshop ID: 3648051123
Mod ID: InjuredZombiesStumble
Enabled on server: yes (B42 — verify MP compatibility)

Zombie behavior tweak: zombies that have taken damage stumble and limp based on their injuries, instead of running at full speed. Makes combat feel more visceral — and gives survivors a chance against already-wounded zeds.

## Items
**No addable items.** Pure zombie behavior modifier.

## Notes
- Affects all zombies globally — applies to existing zombies retroactively
- Likely sandbox options for stumble intensity / limp duration
- Doesn't affect zombie spawn rates or counts — only their movement when injured

## Use Cases (SIMON voice)
- **Combat flavor**: SIMON can reference wounded zeds — "Hear that one dragging itself? Finish it before its friends catch up."
- **Horde aftermath**: when SIMON triggers a horde, the survivors notice injured zeds are easier to kite
- **"Don't get cocky" warnings**: even stumbling zeds can grab you — SIMON can flag a wounded zed as still-dangerous
- **Recon narrative**: survivors can scout ahead more safely if they focus fire on zeds first
