# ResearchLabInternProfession - Zombie Virus Vaccine [B42.14–B42.20]

Workshop ID: 3615135168
Mod ID: ResearchLabInternProfession
**⚠️ Actual title: "Zombie Virus Vaccine"** — listed in server ini as `ResearchLabInternProfession` because that's the mod folder name, but the mod itself is the **full vaccine/cure development system**.

Enabled on server: yes (B42.14–B42.20, MP supported from B42.13+)

This is **THE cure mod on the server**. Custom lab equipment workstations, unique research items, full vaccine + cure development system. Players perform autopsies, collect blood samples + infected brains, extract human bones for anatomical models, find rare research books in military research sites / St. Peregrin Hospital / Louisville university lab, and synthesize vaccines (or maybe a cure).

## ⚠️ CRITICAL FOR SIMON'S "ZOMBIE CURE" NARRATIVE

This mod is the in-universe justification for any cure-related story beat SIMON runs.

### What it adds to gameplay
- **Laboratory equipment** as vanilla-like workstations
- **Blood testing system** — you can now tell if a scratch just gave you an expiration date
- **Progressive research mechanics** — autopsy → blood samples → infected brains → bone extraction → anatomical models → vaccine synthesis
- **Rare research books** unlock procedures (only found in military research sites, hospitals, university labs)
- **Dynamic XP** influenced by profession, perks, and sample results
- **B42-compatible Research Laboratory Intern profession** (the mod is its own profession; works without it)

### SIMON story implications
- Survivors can **actually develop a cure** if they commit to the research arc
- SIMON can frame the cure quest as "long-term goal — not a free pass, you earn it"
- Anti-zombie serum / Knox Antidote narrative (cross-mod with DeadMansDossier) is *diegetically supported* by this mod
